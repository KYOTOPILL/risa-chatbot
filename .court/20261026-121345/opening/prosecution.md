1. **Ten passing evals cannot certify an HR answerer.** The record says "live evals passed 10/10 (commit c918d45)" and "the eval set is 10 questions". A 10/10 result on 10 questions is compatible with a true failure rate of 25% or more. Nothing shows the questions cover all four domains (leave, travel allowance, scholarship, expense forms), Thai-language phrasing, or questions with no answer. The tests were also tuned in the same commit series as the fixes (prompt wording, NOT_FOUND marker). Cost: employees get confidently wrong leave or money answers, and HR finds out from complaints.

2. **Every retrieval threshold is a hand-set guess.** "All RAG settings are hardcoded constants... tuned by hand, not automatic": CHUNK_SIZE 800, TOP_K 4, MAX_DISTANCE 0.6, FILE_MARGIN 0.15. No measurement of recall or precision is cited. The record says the Thai guard "lets a half-Thai reply through", and that is the code's own admission. A policy chunk dropped by MAX_DISTANCE 0.6 produces NOT_FOUND or a hallucination. Cost: silent wrong answers on exactly the policy details people ask about.

3. **The corpus is unstable and the answers can be stale.** The working tree has deleted files, among them "leave-policy-en.docx", "leave-policy-th.pdf", "travel-allowance-2024.txt" and "travel-allowance-2026.txt". One scholarship PDF is untracked. The record does not say which version the index holds, or whether 2024 and 2026 allowance rules can both be retrieved. The record also does not say how a PDF with "Thai private-use glyphs" is checked for garbled text. Cost: the bot quotes a superseded allowance rate as current policy.

4. **It is not deployable as described.** "The admin page has no authentication", so anyone who can reach it can delete or replace HR policy documents and trigger a re-index. "HR_CONTACT... is still a placeholder (hr@example.com)", so the fallback for every unanswered question sends employees to a dead address. Cost: tampered policy content, and users who cannot reach a human when the bot fails.

5. **Capacity is unknown.** The record has "no measured latency; target server (possibly CPU-only) not sized". A qwen3:8b model with bge-m3 on CPU can be too slow for interactive use, and the record has no figure either way. Cost: users abandon the tool, or the project needs new hardware after the commitment is made.

The verdict "good enough to rely on" is premature on all five points. The record supports "working prototype" and nothing stronger.
